# Excluded parties

This file lists the Excluded Parties of the SBO License, version by version.
For each version, Appendix A of that version's License text is the list that
applies. This file repeats it so that the list can be read on its own, and
keeps the history. If this file and Appendix A differ, Appendix A applies.

Section 0 of the License defines who else is excluded with each listed
party: items (b) to (e) of the definition of "Excluded Party", and the
definition of "Excluded Service". Section 13 says what the exclusion covers.

## Version 1.0

Added in version 1.0, 15 November 2026. The entry reads as in Appendix A:

1. AyakaMods, under that name or any other name, and any company or
   organization that owns it or runs its business. A provider that only
   hosts, carries, stores or takes payments for AyakaMods, such as a
   hosting, network, payment, Patreon or Discord provider, is not covered as
   its owner or runner; what such a provider does for AyakaMods is covered
   as item (d) of the definition of "Excluded Party" and item 2 of the
   definition of "Excluded Service" state. The following are Excluded
   Services under this entry:

   a) the website at ayakamods.com, and any other site or domain through
   which the same platform or mod catalog is offered;

   b) its Patreon page at https://www.patreon.com/cw/AyakaMods and any other
   membership or donation page that it runs, its Discord servers, and its
   other accounts, pages and channels on any service;

   c) any launcher, mod manager, client, application, installer, browser
   extension, executable, script, bot, overlay or other program, current or
   future and under any name, that lets people download, install, upload,
   share, manage, load, launch or run mods, or find, join or host sessions
   of a modded game, and that AyakaMods, or anyone that items (b) to (e) of
   the definition of "Excluded Party" add to it, makes, runs, commissions,
   owns, controls, funds, distributes, bundles, sponsors or promotes,
   whether they built it or someone else built it for them, together with
   the catalog, servers, update channels and accounts that feed it; and

   d) any platform, program or service that item (e) of the definition of
   "Excluded Party" describes for AyakaMods. People who move from AyakaMods
   to another service on their own, and authors who publish their mods
   again elsewhere, do not make that service one.

   A program under (c) that does not exist on the date of this version is
   covered from the day it is first made, run, commissioned, funded,
   distributed or promoted as (c) describes.

   A program that was not made for AyakaMods, such as a web browser, a mod
   loader or another site's mod manager, is not covered by (c), and neither
   are its catalog, servers, update channels or accounts, because AyakaMods
   mentions, recommends, links to or promotes it, offers or distributes
   unchanged copies of it, or calls it its own, official, partner or
   sponsored program. Such a program is still covered by (c) as a whole if a
   person or organization under items (a) to (e) of the definition of
   "Excluded Party" owns, controls or funds it, or has a share in its
   revenue; if such a person or organization gets payment, users, data or
   any other benefit from it or for promoting it, under an agreement or
   arrangement, formal or not, with whoever makes or runs it; or if whoever
   makes or runs it agrees, formally or not, to its being presented as
   AyakaMods' own, official, partner or sponsored program. Otherwise it is
   covered only for the copies and builds of it that such a person or
   organization makes, changes, configures or bundles; for the extensions,
   plug-ins, modules and settings for it that are made to work with the
   platform, catalog or accounts that this entry covers, whoever made them,
   or that such a person or organization supplies; and to the extent that it
   gets mods from, or sends mods to, that platform, catalog or accounts, as
   item 3 of the definition of "Excluded Service" states. A program is made
   for AyakaMods when it is made at the request of, or under an agreement
   with, a person or organization under items (a) to (e) of that definition,
   or is made to work only or mainly with the platform, catalog or accounts
   that this entry covers, whoever made it.

## How a party is added or removed

Only a new version of the SBO License adds or removes a party (section 14).
The Licensor signs each version and publishes it, with its Appendix A, at
the publication address and in the Stellar Blade Online source repository
(section 14). For version 1.0 both are
https://github.com/MotherSphere/stellar-blade-coop. The new version gets a
new number, its Appendix A carries the new list, and this file gets a new
section for that version. A party listed in one version
stays listed in every later version, unless the later version removes it by
name in a list of changes published with it.

A party added in a later version is excluded only from works released under
that version or a later one. A work released under "1.0 or later" can still
be used under version 1.0 by a party that version 1.0 does not list, because
the rights granted under each version are irrevocable (section 2).

The Licensor may publish, at the publication address, notices that name
further domains, accounts, programs, companies or successors that it
considers covered by an entry above. Such a notice adds no party (Appendix
A), but nobody may convey a covered work to anyone it names (section 13).
